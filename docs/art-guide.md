# Art guide

- **Style:** isometric pixel art in full color, nearest-neighbour filtering, no anti-aliasing. Shade in steps, never with smooth gradients; ordered dithering between steps is fine.
- **Colors:** each material has a ramp, dark to light, at the top of `art/gen_art.gd`. Ramps shift hue: shadows lean cool, highlights warm. Reuse a ramp, or add one for a new material.
- **Light:** comes from the top left. Characters get a 1 px dark outline and a translucent ground shadow.
- **Tiles:** 128×64 px diamonds (2:1). `assets/tiles.png` has one row per ground (the `atlas` field of `data/tiles/<id>.json`) and 16 columns, one per corner mask of the dual grid (`client/dual_grid.gd`: `NW << 3 | NE << 2 | SW << 1 | SE`, where NW is the top corner on screen, NE the right, SW the left and SE the bottom; column 0 is empty, column 15 is the full tile). Tile size and atlas layout are set in one place, `client/iso_layer.gd`. A tile must join seamlessly with copies of itself: make textures periodic over the tile lattice (see `_lattice` and `_wrap` in `art/gen_art.gd`). Masked tiles cut the full one along a ragged edge that only depends on the two corners of each tile edge, so neighbours always meet.
- **Full tile variants:** after the 16 mask columns, a row holds the other versions of its full tile (`full_variants` in `data/tiles/<id>.json`, up to `IsoLayer.MAX_FULL_VARIANTS`; column `IsoLayer.atlas_column(15, v)`). Each drawn full tile picks one from a hash of its position. Variants must join each other and the transition tiles without a seam: keep the texels along the tile edges identical to variant 0 (`gen_art.gd` blends to variant 0 near the edges), change the inside. `mask_variants` is reserved for versions of the transition tiles and must stay 1 for now.
- **Objects:** one PNG per object, named by the `sprite` field of `data/objects/<id>.json`, so any of them can be replaced alone. The object's cell centre is `IsoLayer.OBJECT_FOOT_PX` px above the bottom edge of the image, horizontally centred.
- **Decals:** small flat details over the ground (tufts, pebbles, flowers, leaves), one PNG per decal named by the `sprite` field of `data/decals/<id>.json`, drawn centred on their position. They lie flat: no outline, at most a faint shadow at the bottom, nothing taller than a few pixels above the ground line, since players and objects never hide behind them. Keep them small (a fraction of a tile) and close to the ground's colours, so they break the pattern without drawing the eye.
- **Characters:** 64×96 px frames, feet at the bottom, centered. `assets/player.png` has 4 columns (facing down, left, right, up) and 5 rows: standing, then a walk cycle of 4 frames over two steps (`WalkAnim.WALK_FRAMES`; poses in `POSES` in `art/gen_art.gd`).
- **Scale:** the camera shows art at 1 texel per screen pixel (zoom 1). Draw details at this size; do not upscale smaller art.
- **Format:** PNG, RGBA, no metadata you would not want public.
- **Generated art:** prefer extending `art/gen_art.gd` (regenerate with `make art`). Images from other tools are fine with a full `ASSETS.toml` entry.
